# Contributing

100 Lessons records real attempts, not polished success stories.

A contribution should explain the problem, context, what was tried, what failed, evidence, what worked, the lesson, and what remains unknown.

## Never submit
Passwords, API keys, authentication tokens, private personal data, confidential employer/client information, copyrighted material you cannot republish, or information you do not have permission to make public.

## Review rule
Submissions are proposals. Nothing becomes a published lesson automatically. Evidence and permissions should be checked first. AI-generated claims must not be presented as verified evidence without an independent source.

## Durable data
Published lessons should remain exportable to open formats and understandable without access to a specific AI model or vendor.
